The Pharmacy Claims Diagnosis Blind-Spot & Attribution Heuristics

In US retail and specialty pharmacy claims (NCPDP standard), prescriptions rarely contain an ICD-10 diagnosis code. For multi-indication biologics (e.g., Humira, Stelara, Dupixent across Rheumatology, Dermatology, and Gastroenterology), commercial teams must infer indication:

1. Deterministic Rule Waterfall

$$\text{Rx} \longrightarrow \text{Matched Dx } [-90, +30\text{d}] \longrightarrow \text{HCP Taxonomy} \longrightarrow \text{Induction Dosing}$$

Rigid Hierarchy: Prioritizes medical claim linkage first. If no medical claim exists, falls back to prescriber NPI primary specialty, then dosing schedule.

2. Naive Bayes Soft Attribution

$$P(I_k \mid \mathbf{x}) \propto P(I_k) \prod_{j=1}^D P(x_j \mid I_k)$$

Probabilistic Allocation: Computes posterior indication probabilities, properly handling multi-morbid patients with dual diagnoses (e.g., Psoriasis + Psoriatic Arthritis).
