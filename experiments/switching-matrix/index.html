Markov Chains, Mover-Stayer Dynamics & Bayesian Credible Fans

Therapy switching between competing brands in a therapeutic class is modeled as a discrete-time stochastic process:

1st-Order Markov Transition Matrix ($\mathbf{P}$)

$$\mathbf{s}_{t+1} = \mathbf{s}_t \mathbf{P}, \qquad \mathbf{s}_k = \mathbf{s}_0 \mathbf{P}^k$$

Transition Matrix: Each row $i$ represents current drug possession, and columns $j$ represent state transitions next month. Row sums must satisfy $\sum_{j=1}^m P_{ij} = 1.0$.

Mover-Stayer Model & Dirichlet Uncertainty

$$\mathbf{P} = \text{diag}(\mathbf{S}) + \text{diag}(\mathbf{1}-\mathbf{S})\mathbf{M}, \qquad \mathbf{P}_i \sim \text{Dirichlet}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_i)$$

Clinical Reality: A core fraction $\mathbf{S}$ of patients are "Stayers" who will never switch due to disease stabilization. Sampling $\mathbf{P}$ from Dirichlet distributions produces Bayesian credible intervals.
