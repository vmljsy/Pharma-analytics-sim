Episode Building & Overlap Resolution

Simulate how discrete prescription dispensing claims transform into continuous clinical treatment episodes under stockpiling, truncation, and gap tolerance rules

From Discrete Claims to Continuous Episodes

In longitudinal pharmacy claims (LRX), patients do not record daily pill consumption; they record discrete dispensing transactions $(t_i, d_i)$ where $t_i$ is the dispensing day and $d_i$ is days of supply. To measure real-world persistence and therapy duration, analysts convert discrete claims into continuous treatment episodes using two core rules:

1. Overlap Handling: When an early refill occurs at $t_{i} \le t_{i-1} + d_{i-1}$, how should remaining pills be handled?

  • Push-Forward (Stockpiling): Patient accumulates supply. New fill begins at $t_{i}^{\text{eff}} = \max(t_i, t_{i-1}^{\text{eff}} + d_{i-1})$.
  • Truncation (Overwrite): Early refill implies dose escalation or discarded old supply. Prior fill is truncated at $t_{i}$.
  • Parallel (Concurrent): Fills run concurrently, creating dual coverage days.

2. Allowable Gap Tolerance ($G_{\text{allowed}}$): If the gap between supply exhaustion and the next refill $\Delta t = t_i - \text{End}_{i-1} \le G_{\text{allowed}}$, the gap is bridged into the same episode. If $\Delta t \gt G_{\text{allowed}}$, the episode terminates due to discontinuation.
