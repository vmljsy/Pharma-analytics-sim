Multi-Market Claims, Advanced Restrictions & Treatment Episodes

In longitudinal real-world data (LRX/RWD), patients frequently receive medications across multiple therapeutic market segments (e.g. GLP-1 RA, SGLT-2i, DPP-4i, Basal Insulin, Dual Incretin, Metformin). Converting discrete claims into continuous treatment episodes requires handling both fill timing and clinical market compatibility:

1. Overlap Handling Physics:
  • Push-Forward (Stockpiling): Patient accumulates supply of the same market segment. Refills start at $t_i^{\text{eff}} = \max(t_i, t_{i-1}^{\text{eff}} + d_{i-1})$.
  • Truncation (Overwrite): Early refill implies dosage escalation or discarded pills. Prior supply truncates at $t_i$.
  • Parallel (Concurrent): Multi-market fills run concurrently, establishing combination coverage.
2. Advanced Market Restrictions:
  • Synergistic Overlap: Guideline-approved combination regimens (e.g. SGLT-2i + GLP-1 RA) provide additive cardio-renal benefits.
  • Therapeutic Duplication: Contraindicated overlapping targets (e.g. GLP-1 RA + DPP-4i) share redundant incretin pathways and trigger restriction conflicts.
  • Interactive Handles & Data Grid: Push, pull, and drag episode boundaries directly on the timeline or inline in the data table.

Allowable Gap Tolerance ($G_{\text{allowed}}$): If the gap between supply exhaustion and the next refill $\Delta t = t_i - \text{End}_{i-1} \le G_{\text{allowed}}$, the gap is bridged into the continuous episode. If $\Delta t \gt G_{\text{allowed}}$, the episode terminates due to discontinuation.
