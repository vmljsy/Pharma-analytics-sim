From Written Rx to Paid Dispense: The Adjudication State Machine

In the United States, commercial health plans and Pharmacy Benefit Managers (PBMs) enforce utilization management edits before a pharmacy claim is paid:

1. Utilization Management (PA & Step Therapy)

$$\text{Written Rx} \xrightarrow{\text{NCPDP Rejection}} \text{PA Required} \xrightarrow{T_{\text{PA}} \sim \text{Weibull}(\lambda, k)} \text{Approved} \mid \text{Denied}$$

Turnaround Friction: Each additional day of prior authorization review accelerates patient prescription abandonment. If turnaround exceeds $7$ days, prescription abandonment triples.

2. Out-of-Pocket Copay Elasticity

$$P(\text{Abandon} \mid \text{Copay}) = 1 - \frac{1}{1 + \left(\frac{\text{Copay}}{\text{EC}_{50}}\right)^\gamma}$$

The Fill Boundary: Even after successful payer approval, copays exceeding $\$50$ trigger rapid prescription reversal and counter abandonment.
