PDC vs MPR, CMA Taxonomy & Latent Trajectory Clusters

Adherence analytics encompass both single-patient dispensary bitmasks and longitudinal population mixture models.

PDC (PQA Standard) vs CMA Taxonomy

$$\text{PDC} = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^T \mathbb{I}(\text{Day } t \text{ covered})}{T} \times 100\%$$

CMA7 vs CMA8: CMA7 pushes overlapping days forward inside the observation window. CMA8 truncates early-supply carryover at the initiation boundary ($t_0$).

Group-Based Trajectory Modeling (GBTM)

$$P(Y_i) = \sum_{j=1}^J \pi_j P(Y_i \mid \text{Group } j, \boldsymbol{\beta}_j)$$

Latent Clusters: Rather than forcing patients into binary $80\%$ cutoff, GBTM discovers distinct behavioral archetypes: Consistent Adherers, Gradual Decliners, Gap-and-Restart, and Early Discontinuers.
