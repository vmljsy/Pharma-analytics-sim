Confounding by Indication, Propensity Scores & Target Trial Emulation

In observational real-world claims, treatment assignment is not randomized. Sicker, older, and multi-morbid patients are selectively channeled into newer second-line therapies (Brand Alpha), producing severe bias:

1. The Confounding Trap (Naive KM)

$$\text{Naive HR} \ne \text{Causal Effect} \quad (\text{Omitted Variable Bias})$$

Because Brand Alpha patients have higher baseline Charlson Comorbidity scores and frailty, unadjusted survival curves falsely imply that Brand Alpha is inferior or equivalent to standard of care.

2. IPTW & Target Trial Emulation (CCW)

$$w_i = \frac{Z_i}{e_i} + \frac{1 - Z_i}{1 - e_i}, \qquad e_i = P(Z_i = 1 \mid \mathbf{X}_i)$$

Pseudo-Population Balancing: Propensity score weighting creates a synthetic balanced cohort where covariate distributions match ($|\text{SMD}| < 0.1$), unveiling the true causal treatment benefit.
