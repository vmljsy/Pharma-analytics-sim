Longitudinal Source of Business (SoB) Classification

Pharma commercial teams cannot rely solely on gross prescription volume; understanding where patients originate dictates commercial strategy, market share defense, and physician detailing. At every brand initiation (Index Claim at $t_{\text{index}}$), we evaluate patient history within a lookback window $[t_{\text{index}} - W_{\text{lookback}}, t_{\text{index}})$:

1. New to Brand (NTB / Naive)

No prior therapy in lookback window. Represents market expansion and naive treatment initiation.

2. Switch-In

Discontinued a competitor therapy within switch window $\Delta t \le W_{\text{switch}}$. Direct competitive conquest.

3. Add-On / Combination

Initiated brand while continuing active background competitor therapy without discontinuation.

4. Restart / Re-initiation

Prior brand user who experienced a treatment gap $\Delta t > W_{\text{restart}}$ and is now resuming therapy.
