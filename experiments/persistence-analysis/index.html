Kaplan-Meier Estimator, Greenwood Variance & Mixture Cure Models

In real-world observational persistence studies, treatment discontinuation is modeled as a time-to-event failure process with administrative and loss-to-follow-up right-censoring:

Kaplan-Meier Product-Limit Estimator

$$\hat{S}(t) = \prod_{t_i \le t} \left( 1 - \frac{d_i}{n_i} \right), \qquad \widehat{\text{SE}}(\hat{S}(t)) = \hat{S}(t) \sqrt{ \sum_{t_i \le t} \frac{d_i}{n_i (n_i - d_i)} }$$

Permissible Gap Sensitivity: A missed refill of 45 days is a "Discontinuation" under a strict 30d gap rule, but "Continuous" under a 60d gap rule.

Parametric Extrapolation & Mixture Cure Models

$$S(t) = \pi + (1-\pi)S_u(t), \qquad \text{RMST}(\tau) = \int_0^\tau S(t) \, dt$$

HEOR Horizon Modeling: Parametric curves (Weibull, Log-Logistic, Gompertz) project long-term survival beyond clinical trial horizons. Cure fraction $\pi$ captures permanent responders.
