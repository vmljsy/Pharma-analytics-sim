The Economics of Prescriber Skew & Sales Force Allocation

In pharmaceutical commercial analytics (e.g. IQVIA NPA / Xponent data), prescriber writing behavior follows an extreme power-law distribution. Unlike consumer retail, a small fraction of healthcare professionals (HCPs) drives the vast majority of total prescription volume (TRx/NRx).

Lorenz Concentration Curve & Gini Coefficient

$$\text{Gini} = 1 - 2 \int_0^1 L(p) \, dp = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^N (2i - N - 1) y_i}{N \sum_{i=1}^N y_i}$$

The Pharma 80/20 Reality: While general wealth has a Gini around $0.4$, pharmaceutical specialty markets exhibit extreme Gini coefficients of $0.65$ to $0.88$. Often, the top $15\%\text{--}20\%$ of prescribers generate over $80\%$ of total brand therapy volume.

Deciles, Quintiles & Reach-Frequency (R&F)

$$\text{Required Reps (FTE)} = \frac{\sum_{d \ge d_{\text{min}}} N_d \times \text{Freq}_d \times \text{Reach}_d}{\text{Rep Monthly Call Capacity}}$$

SFE Call Planning: Doctors are binned into Deciles (1 to 10) or Quintiles (1 to 5). Tier 10/9 high-writers receive intensive personal detailing (2–3 calls/month), whereas lower tiers (D1–D4) are addressed via omnichannel non-personal promotion (NPP).
