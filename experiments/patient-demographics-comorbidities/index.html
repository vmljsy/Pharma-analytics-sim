Demographic Pyramids, Payer Channels & Charlson Comorbidity Index (CCI)

In real-world data (RWD) claims analysis (Optum, MarketScan, Medicare FFS, Symphony), patient outcomes are fundamentally governed by baseline demographic characteristics, insurance channel eligibility, and multimorbidity burden.

Charlson Comorbidity Index (CCI) Scoring

$$\text{CCI} = \sum_{k=1}^K w_k \cdot x_k, \qquad w_k \in \{1, 2, 3, 6\}$$

ICD-10 Clinical Weighting: Mild conditions (MI, CHF, COPD, Diabetes) receive weight $+1$; end-organ damage (complicated diabetes, moderate/severe CKD, hemiplegia) receives $+2$; severe liver disease receives $+3$; and metastatic malignancy receives $+6$.

Multimorbidity Risk Quintiles & The Age 65 Cliff

$$J(A, B) = \frac{|A \cap B|}{|A \cup B|}, \qquad \text{PDC}(Q) = \text{PDC}_0 - \beta \cdot \text{CCI}_Q$$

Polypharmacy & Adherence: As patients progress into higher risk quintiles ($Q_4\text{--}Q_5$), regimen complexity increases exponentially. This clinical friction drives severe drops in 12-month Proportion of Days Covered (PDC) and increases hospitalization hazard.
