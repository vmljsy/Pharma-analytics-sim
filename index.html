Pharma Real-World Data (RWD) Engineering Standards

This laboratory implements industry-standard health economics and outcomes research (HEOR) algorithms formulated by the Pharmacy Quality Alliance (PQA), ISPOR, and NCCN Guidelines.

Deterministic Push-Forward

Accounts for pill stockpiling by advancing future start dates to the exhaustion date of previous fills without double-counting.

Right-Censored Survival

Kaplan-Meier product-limit estimators strictly segregate true discontinuation events from administrative study enrollment cutoffs.

Zero-Dependency Engine

Pure ES6 modular architecture executing instantaneously in browser WebAssembly / JavaScript runtime with Plotly visualization.
